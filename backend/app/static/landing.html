Google Drive

How your data is handled.

Aruvi uses the Google Drive API only for backups you explicitly request.

Drive scope: drive.file

Aruvi requests the minimum Google Drive scope (drive.file), which allows us to upload only the files and folders you explicitly create through the service. We do not access, read, or modify any other files in your Google Drive.

You can revoke access at any time via Google Account Permissions. No data is shared with third parties and no analytics or tracking is used.
